Privacy policy

K. Martin is responsible for the personal data described here. We store player names, match settings, scores, results, statistics and timestamps. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing shared scoring and match history. We do not use match data for advertising or visitor analytics.

Player names may be entered by you or another participant. You can use a nickname instead of your real name. If you enter information about other participants, let them know about this policy. Anyone with a match's link or ID can view, change or delete that match, so share it only with people you trust.

The website is hosted in the Netherlands, and match data is stored using MongoDB Atlas in Germany. Matches are kept indefinitely for match history, unless deleted. We do not create separate database backups.

Our web-server and application logs may contain your IP address and request or error information. We use these for troubleshooting and security, based on our legitimate interest in keeping the service working. These logs are configured to retain around two weeks of archives, although inactive logs may remain longer.

Cloudflare handles website traffic, including IP addresses and request information, to help deliver and protect the service. Fonts and icons are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser request information when these resources are loaded.

These providers may process some data in other countries, including the US. You can read more about how these services handle your data here: Cloudflare, MongoDB and Google.

If you have questions about your data, or want to see, correct or delete it, email dartsmatcherapp@gmail.com. You can also ask us to limit how we use your data or object to its use. Privacy complaints can also be made to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, the Dutch privacy regulator.

Cookies and local storage

Darts Matcher uses localStorage and IndexedDB in your browser to remember your theme preference, which players you are scoring for, and references to matches you have visited. These support the website's functionality.

These browser records have no automatic expiry. They remain until you remove them through the available controls, clear the website's browser data, or your browser removes them.

Clearing the website's data through your browser settings resets saved preferences and removes your local match-history references. It does not delete matches from the server. Blocking browser storage may prevent settings and match history from working correctly.

The server may set a session cookie for technical request handling. Cloudflare may also use security cookies to detect automated traffic or remember that your browser has passed a security check. More information is available in Cloudflare's cookie documentation.
